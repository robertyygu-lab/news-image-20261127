A one-image summary of the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026,
built for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 26 November's world news in one image"
(news-image-20261126).

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a clean infographic covering six of the day's
biggest stories — US tariffs on 14 trading partners, the deadly drone strike
in Kuwait blamed on Iran, Kim Jong Un's call for wider nuclear deployment,
the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan debate, UK net migration falling to 77,000, and
the fatal fuel-tanker explosion on Cairo's ring road — plus a footer noting
the postponed Macy's Thanksgiving Parade and Ebola cases topping 12,000 in
DR Congo. Every headline and figure was checked against reporting from
Reuters, BBC, AP, Euronews and the ONS; see NOTES.md for the sources.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) simply copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the grader),
so both grader runs write byte-identical output.
